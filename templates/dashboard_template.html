Application Desk

Indeed + LinkedIn · 5 regions

"Confidence: reviewed" means the full job description was checked before scoring. "Title only" jobs were scored on title/role heuristics alone — give those an extra look before applying. Salary is rarely listed by Indeed or LinkedIn; treat "salary not listed" as unverified, not absent. Fit scoring assumes 1 year full-time + 9 months part-time/internship experience, so roles asking well above that are scored down. Postings older than 3 weeks are filtered out as likely closed. Once a listing appears here it's recorded and won't be shown again in a future batch. Marking a job "Applied" moves it out of the region lists into the Applied tab above, permanently, across every country and every day — it's saved in this browser and survives page refreshes and future updates to this same dashboard link. To get a resume file for an application: "Open resume in new tab" opens it standalone and triggers your browser's print dialog automatically — choose "Save as PDF" as the destination. If your browser blocks the new tab, use "Download resume (HTML)" instead, then open that file and print/save as PDF from there. Applying itself always opens the real listing in a new tab — nothing here submits anything automatically.
