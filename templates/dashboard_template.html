Application Desk

Indeed + LinkedIn · 5 regions

"Confidence: reviewed" means the full job description was checked before scoring. "Title only" jobs were scored on title/role heuristics alone — give those an extra look before applying. Salary is rarely listed by Indeed or LinkedIn; treat "salary not listed" as unverified, not absent. Fit scoring assumes 1 year full-time + 9 months part-time/internship experience, so roles asking well above that are scored down. Postings older than 3 weeks are treated as likely closed and hidden from the New/All lists automatically — this is re-checked live every time you open this page (against the posting's original date), not just when it was first added, so a job that goes stale after sitting here a while will quietly drop out of view on its own. Applied jobs are the one exception: they stay visible in the Applied tab forever regardless of age, since that's your record of what you did, not a live listing. Once a listing appears here it's recorded and won't be shown again in a future batch. Marking a job "Applied" moves it out of the region lists into the Applied tab above, permanently, across every country and every day — it's saved in this browser and survives page refreshes and future updates to this same dashboard link. Clicking "Not relevant" on a job asks for a short reason, then moves it into the Not relevant tab and out of New/All — same permanent, per-browser storage as Applied. This is only useful long-term if Claude actually sees the reasons: open the Not relevant tab and use "Save as file" or "Copy to clipboard" to export them, then paste that back into a future chat so recurring rejection reasons (wrong seniority, wrong domain, recruiter spam, etc.) can get baked into the search and scoring rules instead of you filtering the same kind of listing out by hand every day. Jobs whose posting explicitly states no visa sponsorship / local-candidates-only / must-already-have-right-to-work are flagged with a red No sponsorship badge and scored down heavily — this is a text match against the saved description, so it can only catch what that text actually contains, not what the original listing said if it was condensed out during scoring; always verify on the real posting before ruling a job in or out on this basis alone. Each job now has both a Resume and Cover Letter tab in its preview — both are tailored to that specific job description (resume bullets are reordered to lead with the most relevant experience; the cover letter's story paragraph is auto-matched from a library of real accounts). To get a file for an application: this page's viewer may block in-page downloads/new tabs entirely, so the reliable way is to ask Claude directly in chat — e.g. "send me the resume and cover letter for <job title> at <company>" — and it'll arrive as real PDF files. The "Open in new tab" / "Download (HTML)" buttons here follow whichever tab (resume or cover letter) is currently selected, and are a convenience that works in some browsers, but don't rely on them if nothing happens when clicked. Applying itself always opens the real listing in a new tab — nothing here submits anything automatically.
