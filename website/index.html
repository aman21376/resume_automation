01

Where I sit

Most roadmaps die in the gap between what the business wants and what the data supports. I work in that gap. Three years in, the pattern holds: define the hypothesis, ship the smallest test that can kill it, read the numbers honestly, and only then commit budget or headcount. It's slower than moving on instinct — it's also why a customer-support AI I ran went from -120% gross margin to breakeven, and why the brand partnerships I manage grew 20% month-over-month instead of spiking for a quarter and fading.

Practically, that means I write PRDs like a product manager, query the warehouse like an analyst, and negotiate like the business owner I'm accountable to — because at a 500M-user marketplace, a decision that skips any one of those lenses is a decision made on hope.
